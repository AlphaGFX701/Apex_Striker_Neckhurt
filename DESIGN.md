# Flight deck design
Industrial top-down military spacecraft; Thai mobile-first portrait gameplay. Artist-authored Skorpio artwork, blue friendly engines and red hostile markings. Original and adapted assets are distributed under CC-BY-SA 3.0; see ASSET-CREDITS.md.

Runtime tokens and responsive overrides live in src/style.css. Palette: space #070B12, armor #303B48, text #E6EDF3, friendly #56CFFF, hostile #EF6655, warning #FFC76A. Chakra Petch headings, Prompt body. DOM owns menus, readable HUD and settings; Phaser owns the field. Persistent combat HUD: score/time/lives, boss bar and three skill buttons. Diagnostics disclosed through the settings toggle, which pauses combat. Selected ship is cosmetic; weapon is committed at start.
